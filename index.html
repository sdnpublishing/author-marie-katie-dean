About Marie Katie Dean

MK

Marie Katie Dean

Marie Katie Dean bridges quantum science and forest ecology, exploring how the counterintuitive principles of quantum biology might illuminate the extraordinary behaviours of trees and woodland systems.

Her books are speculative in the best sense — rigorous enough to be credible, imaginative enough to be exciting, and always grounded in the genuine strangeness of what science is actually finding.

For readers at the frontier of the natural sciences.
